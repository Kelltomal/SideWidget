# Code of conduct

These expectations apply to participation in the SideWidget repository, including issues, pull requests, and reviews.

- Treat other participants with respect. Discuss the work and give feedback people can act on.
- Respect people’s privacy and share their personal information only with their consent.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks are grounds for moderation.

To raise a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer privately if you have a suitable channel. Use GitHub’s reporting tools for abusive content or behavior. Maintainers may remove harmful content, lock conversations, or restrict participation. [GitHub’s reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
